Filters combine with AND. Several picks in one filter combine with OR. Changing any filter goes back to page 1. The URL holds the filters and the page, so a copied link opens the same view. Submitted from Sep 1 and Completed by Sep 30 means submitted at or after local midnight on Sep 1 and completed before local midnight on Oct 1. Either date can be set alone. Once Completed by is set, jobs that have not completed drop out. Jobs submitted before this change show "—" under Submitted by; they are not backfilled. Reset filters goes to the bare URL, so Submitted by goes back to you.
